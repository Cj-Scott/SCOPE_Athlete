Private alpha privacy notice

Use synthetic data. Keep real athlete information out of alpha.

SCOPE Athlete is being reviewed as a local-first Windows alpha. This notice explains what stays on your device, what leaves the app, and where feedback goes.

Privacy at a glance Local records stay on your device unless you export them. Feedback goes to Google Forms and is reviewed manually. Do not enter real athlete, family, school-contact, or private planning data. No under-13 reviewer submissions during alpha.
01

Local app records

The desktop alpha stores planning records locally in the Windows user profile. Browser preview mode uses browser storage. These local records are not cloud-synced by SCOPE Athlete.

02

Exports and diagnostics

Backups and diagnostics are local files you choose to export. Diagnostics are designed to redact private fields and keep bounded retention settings.

03

Feedback form

Structured alpha feedback opens a Google Form. Assume feedback may include your response text and basic Google Form metadata. Alpha feedback is targeted for deletion or review within 90 days.

What the alpha handles

Planning records are local-first, not encrypted cloud records.

Records you create in the app

Player profiles, schools, contacts, outreach notes, tasks, video-link references, evidence notes, settings, update metadata, backups, and diagnostics are intended to stay local unless you export them or open an external link.

Plaintext local storage

The current alpha does not add passphrase encryption. Windows account protection, device security, and your chosen backup locations matter. Use synthetic demo data only during alpha review.

Profile images and links

Profile images may be saved as local profile data or image links. Media entries store provider links and notes, not uploaded video files.

What may leave the app

A few actions intentionally use external services.

Google Form feedbackUsed for private alpha feedback. Do not submit real athlete or family data. Responses are reviewed manually before sanitized work becomes GitHub issues.
Update checksThe app checks public GitHub Pages/GitHub endpoints for signed app-update and data-pack metadata.
Media link checksIf you ask the app to check a public video link, it may make a request to that provider URL to see whether the link appears reachable.
External linksSchool, NCAA, admissions, roster, checksum, and feedback links open outside the app or public site.
GitHub Pages/download hostingThe public website and release assets are hosted through GitHub. GitHub may process standard web/download request metadata under its own policies.

Control and retention

You control local records; feedback is handled manually.

Local records

You can reset alpha data from Settings and uninstall the Windows app through Windows Settings. Exported backups or diagnostics stay wherever you save them.

Feedback retention

Alpha Google Form responses are targeted for deletion or review within 90 days. Useful feedback may be converted into sanitized product tasks without copying private reviewer details.

Contact path

For alpha privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Christopher Scott / SCOPE Athlete through the private alpha review channel. A non-personal public contact address should replace this placeholder before broader release.

Attorney review

This is an alpha notice, not legal certification.

This page is intended to make current alpha behavior clear to trusted reviewers. It should be reviewed by qualified counsel before broad public launch, app-store distribution, cloud sync, real minor-athlete data collection, or AI features that process private information.
