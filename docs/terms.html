Private alpha terms / counsel review draft

Review the workflow, not real athlete data.

These alpha terms explain what SCOPE Athlete is, what it is not, and how reviewers should treat the current Windows build and public preview materials.

Alpha boundaries No real athlete or family data. No under-13 reviewer submissions. No guarantee of school attention or outcomes. No NCAA, school, league, club, or governing-body endorsement.
01

Private alpha only

The current experience is for trusted review of product clarity, trust, workflow, and packaging readiness. It is not a production release.

02

Planning support

SCOPE Athlete helps organize school-fit planning. It does not recruit athletes, contact schools for users, or promise attention from schools.

03

Verify sources

Rules, school data, costs, rosters, links, and evidence should be checked at official sources before a family acts on them.

Reviewer use

Use synthetic demo data and keep the alpha controlled.

Allowed review

Watch the public videos, follow one walkthrough, load synthetic demo data when downloads reopen, inspect the workflow, and submit structured feedback.

Not for real records

Do not enter real athlete, family, school-contact, private planning, medical, financial, or sensitive information during alpha review.

Redistribution

Please do not redistribute installers, screenshots, walkthrough clips, or product claims without checking with Christopher Scott / SCOPE Athlete first.

Unsigned Windows alpha

The installer is an alpha artifact.

When downloads reopen, Windows may warn because the alpha installer is not production code-signed. Only install builds from the official SCOPE Athlete public page or GitHub Release, and use published checksums when possible.

Disclaimers and non-affiliation

SCOPE Athlete is not an official school, NCAA, league, or club service.

No outcome guaranteeThe tool does not guarantee coach responses, school interest, offers, admission, roster placement, scholarships, or eligibility outcomes.
No legal or compliance adviceRules and milestones are planning prompts. Verify NCAA, school, admissions, and financial information at official sources before acting.
No endorsementReal school names, public links, logos, NCAA references, or league references do not imply affiliation, sponsorship, approval, or endorsement.
Data may be incompleteCatalog, rules, cost, roster, and evidence information can be incomplete, stale, or wrong. Treat it as a starting point for review.
Feedback useFeedback may be summarized into product work after manual review. Private details should not be copied into public issues.
